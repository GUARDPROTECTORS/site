Advocacy

Raise Awareness: Inform the public about the different types of cybercrimes, how they happen, and how to identify them. The more people know about the threats, the better equipped they will be to avoid falling victim to them.

Promote Digital Safety Practices: Teach individuals simple, practical steps they can take to secure their online accounts and devices. This includes guidance on setting strong passwords, using two-factor authentication, and spotting phishing attempts.

Provide Accessible Resources: Offer tools, guides, and tutorials that are easy to understand and use. These resources will help people make smarter, safer choices when interacting with the internet and digital technologies.

Create a Supportive Community: Build a space where individuals can share experiences, ask questions, and receive advice on how to deal with cyber threats. The community will also provide access to experts who can help users solve specific security issues.

Advocating for Better Cybersecurity: Encouraging stronger cybersecurity policies and practices to protect people from cybercrime on a larger scale.
